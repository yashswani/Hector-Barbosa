Captain Hector Barbossa

The Greatest Pirate to Ever Sail the Seven Seas

The Legendary Voyage of Hector Barbossa:

  • The Mutiny - Led the uprising against Jack Sparrow to take command of the Black Pearl.
  • The Curse - Spent years as an immortal, unable to feel the wind on his face or the taste of food due to the Aztec Gold.
  • The Resurrection - Brought back from the locker by Tia Dalma to lead the pirate lords.
  • The Privateer - Lost a leg to Blackbeard but gained a commission from King George II.
  • The Final Sacrifice - Gave his life to save his daughter, Carina Smyth, while taking down Salazar.

"For too long I've been parched of thirst and unable to quench it. Too long I've been starving to death and haven't died. I feel nothing. Not the wind on my face nor the spray of the sea. Nor the warmth of a woman's flesh."
